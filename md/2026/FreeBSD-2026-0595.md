# net/rsync -- denial of service
VulnCheck reports:

> rsync through 3.5.1 contains a denial of service vulnerability in the
> delta-transfer sender\'s hash_search() in match.c that allows
> malicious receivers to trigger unbounded strong-checksum work at
> consecutive offsets. Unauthenticated attackers pulling from a public
> daemon module can supply weak checksums colliding with consecutive
> rolling windows plus non-matching strong checksums, exhausting sender
> CPU across concurrent connections.


## Affected packages
 - **rsync**
    - **Fixed:** 3.5.1_1
