# LibreSSL -- Multiple vulnerabilities
The LibreSSL project reports:

> - Ensure verify callbacks always returning 1 can see a hostname
>   mismatch
> - Correct botched size check in dtls1_preprocess_fragment()
> - Limit size of buffered DTLS handshake messages
> - Avoid potential overread on interrupted retransmission in DTLS
> - Fix OCSP responder authorization bypass in libtls and ocspcheck(8)


## Affected packages
 - **libressl**
    - **Fixed:** 4.3.3
 - **libressl-devel**
    - **Fixed:** 4.3.3
