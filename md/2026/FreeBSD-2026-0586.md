# unarr -- multiple vulnerabilities in the rar, zip and tar readers
The unarr project reports:

> - rar: Fix OOB read in rar_conv_unicode_to_utf8
> - rar: Prevent heap overflow when copying filters into RAR VM
> - zip: Fix off-by-3 heap out-of-bounds read in ZIP64 extra field
>   parser
> - tar: Fix off-by-1 heap overflow in tar_get_name when concatenating
>   USTAR filename and prefix at full length


## Affected packages
 - **unarr**
    - **Fixed:** 1.2.0
