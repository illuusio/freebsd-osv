# conduit -- authentication bypass vulnerability
Conduit team reports:

> Fixes a vulnerability where endpoints which required authentication,
> but functionality do not depend on the user/server calling it, could
> have their authentication bypassed, as well as causing crashes for all
> other authenticated endpoints. It is recommended you upgrade ASAP.


## Affected packages
 - **matrix-conduit**
    - **Fixed:** 0.10.13
