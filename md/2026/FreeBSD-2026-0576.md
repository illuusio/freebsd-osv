# uriparser -- Denial of Service through Unbound Recursion
The URIparser project reports:

> uriparser through 0.9.9 allows unbounded recursion and stack
> consumption, as demonstrated by ParseMustBeSegmentNzNc with large
> input containing many commas.


## Affected packages
 - **uriparser**
    - **Fixed:** 1.0.0
