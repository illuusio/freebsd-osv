# dash -- arith: INTMAX_MIN / -1 overflow
https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/utils/dash/dash.git/commit/?id=0034bfe185d3d875cebace8cb3ca5c9dabf9e0f3
reports:

> Division and remainder currently guard against division by zero, but
> not against the signed overflow case INTMAX_MIN / -1. On affected
> systems this can trigger SIGFPE during arithmetic expansion.


## Affected packages
 - **dash**
    - **Fixed:** 0.5.13.3
