# Text::CSV_XS -- multiple vulnerabilities
H.Merijn Brand - Tux \<linux@tux.freedom.nl\> reports:

> Text::CSV_XS versions before 1.65 have these vulnerabilities listed
> below.
>
> - CVE-2026-110759: parse (): report trailing lone CR, do not store EOF
>   as 0xFF
> - CVE-2026-110758: Under allow_whitespace, do not strip beyond field
>   start
> - CVE-2026-110757: combine (): decide quoting from the bytes, not the
>   NV flags
> - CVE-2026-110756: Flush saved bytes in Combine ()
> - CVE-2026-110755: Re-use AHEAD with \\r instead of copying
> - CVE-2026-110754: getline (): free the result array when the parse
>   fails
> - CVE-2026-110753: Do not free the bound column scalar in
>   skip_empty_rows
> - CVE-2026-110752: fragment (): do not size memory by the selector
>   integers


## Affected packages
 - **p5-Text-CSV_XS**
    - **Fixed:** 1.65
