# p5-HTTP-Body -- multiple vulnerabilities
HTTP-Body oroject reports:

> Multiple security issues:
>
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116131: urlencoded bodies are limited to
>   \$HTTP::Body::UrlEncoded::max_params fields and multipart bodies to
>   \$HTTP::Body::MultiPart::max_parts parts (1000 each); reported and
>   patched by CPANSec
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116132: the temporary file of an upload that is
>   still incomplete at DESTROY-time is now always deleted; reported and
>   patched by CPANSec (also fixes RT#84004)
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116133: a multipart body without a boundary or
>   with a malformed delimiter line no longer takes quadratic time; a
>   malformed delimiter line now croaks, and padding or an epilogue
>   after the close delimiter is accepted; reported and patched by
>   CPANSec
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116134: a multipart part\'s header block is
>   limited to \$HTTP::Body::MultiPart::max_header_size bytes (32768)
>   and \$HTTP::Body::MultiPart::max_header_lines lines (100); reported
>   and patched by CPANSec
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116135: mixed-case percent escapes (e.g. %aB) in
>   urlencoded bodies are decoded instead of being dropped; reported and
>   patched by CPANSec
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116285: the chunk size line of a chunked body is
>   matched in linear instead of quadratic time
> - SECURITY, CVE-2026-116531: a chunked body is now framed
>   independently of where the reads feeding add() fall; a read that
>   split a chunk could leave the parser never reaching \'done\',
>   hanging a server that reads until done


## Affected packages
 - **p5-HTTP-Body**
    - **Fixed:** 1.24
