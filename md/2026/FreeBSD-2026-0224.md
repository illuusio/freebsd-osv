# Grafana -- Public dashboards discloses all direct mode datasources
https://grafana.com/security/security-advisories/cve-2026-27877 reports:

> When using public dashboards and direct data-sources, all direct
> data-sources\' passwords are exposed despite not being used in
> dashboards. No passwords of proxied data-sources are exposed. We
> encourage all direct data-sources to be converted to proxied
> data-sources as far as possible to improve your deployments\'
> security.
