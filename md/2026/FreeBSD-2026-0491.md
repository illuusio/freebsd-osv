# Keycloak -- multiple vulnerabilities
The Keycloak Team reports:

> - \[CVE-2026-12388\]: IdP mapper admin role escalation.
> - \[CVE-2026-14781\]: OIDC broker applies id_token email_verified to
>   userinfo email, marking unverified addresses as verified.
> - \[CVE-2026-19608\]: Name-only group claims let same-name groups
>   satisfy path-specific group policies.
> - \[CVE-2026-54515\], \[CVE-2026-59889\]: Third-party dependency fix,
>   com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-databind 2.22.0.
> - \[CVE-2026-59903\]: Third-party dependency fix,
>   io.netty:netty-codec-http:4.1.136.Final.


## Affected packages
 - **keycloak**
    - **Fixed:** 26.8.0
