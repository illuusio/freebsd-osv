# oauth2-proxy -- multiple vulnerabilities
During session resumption in crypto/tls, if the underlying Config has
its ClientCAs or RootCAs fields mutated between the initial handshake
and the resumed handshake, the resumed handshake may succeed when it
should have failed.


## Affected packages
 - **oauth2-proxy**
    - **Fixed:** 7.14.2
