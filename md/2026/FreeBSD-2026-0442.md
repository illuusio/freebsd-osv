# OpenVPN: denial of service
OpenVPN team reports:

> OpenVPN 2.x allows attackers to potentially cause a denial of service
> against the control channel reliability layer, either by driving the
> reliable TLS retransmission timeout to grow without bound, or by
> sending acknowledgements for packets that cannot be outstanding.


## Affected packages
 - **openvpn**
    - **Fixed:** 2.7.7
