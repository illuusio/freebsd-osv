# Krill -- multiple vulnerabilities
NLnet Labs reports:

> - CVE-2026-109140: Krill login-session tokens reuse their encryption
>   nonce across restarts, so any user who can log in can forge an
>   \"admin\" token.
> - CVE-2026-109141: Krill users restricted to one CA can make any other
>   CA delegate its prefixes and publish ROAs for them via bulk import.
> - CVE-2026-109142: Krill treats a \"local parent\" as
>   already-authenticated, letting a CA operator act as any child of any
>   CA on the same server.


## Affected packages
 - **krill**
    - **Fixed:** 0.16.1
