# Mozilla -- multiple vulnerabilities
Memory safety bugs present in firefox-esr 140.6, Thunderbird ESR 140.6,
Firefox 146 and Thunderbird 146.

Spoofing issue in the DOM: Copy & Paste and Drag & Drop component.

Clickjacking issue and information disclosure in the PDF Viewer
component.

Use-after-free in the JavaScript: GC component.

Use-after-free in the JavaScript Engine component.

Information disclosure in the Networking component.

Sandbox escape due to incorrect boundary conditions in the Graphics:
CanvasWebGL component.


## Affected packages
 - **firefox**
    - **Fixed:** 147.0.0,2
 - **firefox-esr**
    - **Fixed:** 140.7.0,2
 - **thunderbird**
    - **Fixed:** 147
