# NSD -- multiple vulnerabilities
NLnet Labs reports:

> - CVE-2026-18664: IP range access control restrictions are bypassed
>   for some unintended IP. Reported by [Qifan Zhang from Palo Alto
>   Networks](https://qifanz.com/), [Claude](https://claude.ai) and [Ada
>   Logics](https://adalogics.com/).
> - CVE-2026-18916: Any remote client can denial TCP service by
>   throttling the TCP receive window (down to 1). Reported by [akoul
>   (Akhil Koul)](https://github.com/akoul)
> - CVE-2026-19401: Any remote client can denial UDP service by sending
>   a specifically crafted query with multiple DNS Cookie options.
>   Reported by [Qifan Zhang from Palo Alto
>   Networks](https://qifanz.com/) and
>   [afldl](mailto:zhangph@yandex.com).
> - CVE-2026-19538: Anyone with access to the proxy protocol port over
>   TCP or TLS can bypass BLOCKED access control items. Reported by
>   [Qifan Zhang from Palo Alto Networks](https://qifanz.com/).


## Affected packages
 - **nsd**
    - **Fixed:** 4.15.1
