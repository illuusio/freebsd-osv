# MySQL 26.7 -- Multiple vulnerabilities, no fix available
Oracle reports:

> The October 2026 Critical Patch Update contains 98 new security
> patches for Oracle MySQL, 29 of which may be remotely exploitable
> without authentication.
>
> MySQL 26.7 is an Innovation release. It reached its end of life on
> 2026-10-20, when MySQL 26.10 was released, and Oracle ships no patched
> 26.7 release. Users have to move to MySQL 26.10 or to one of the LTS
> series, 8.4 or 9.7.
>
> The list below includes the MySQL Router issues, because the router
> binaries are shipped by mysql267-server.
>
> Two further issues listed by Oracle, in the bundled curl and OpenSSL
> copies, do not apply to these ports: they build against the curl and
> OpenSSL of the system.


## Affected packages
 - **mysql267-client**
    - **Introduced:** 26.7.0
 - **mysql267-server**
    - **Introduced:** 26.7.0
