# Grafana -- Public Dashboards time range restriction on annotations can be bypassed
https://grafana.com/security/security-advisories/cve-2026-21722 reports:

> Public dashboards with annotations enabled did not limit their
> annotation timerange to the locked timerange of the public dashboard.
> This means one could read the entire history of annotations visible on
> the specific dashboard, even those outside the locked timerange. This
> did not leak any annotations that would not otherwise be visible on
> the public dashboard.
