# Erlang/OTP -- erl_tar denial of service via crafted GNU sparse member
https://github.com/erlang/otp/security/advisories/GHSA-phm5-m6cj-5382
reports:

> A loop with unreachable exit condition (\'infinite loop\')
> vulnerability in the erl_tar module allowed an attacker who supplied a
> tar archive to cause a denial of service via a crafted GNU sparse
> member with a trailing hole.


## Affected packages
 - **erlang-runtime29**
    - **Fixed:** 29.1.2
