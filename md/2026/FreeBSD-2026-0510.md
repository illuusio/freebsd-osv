# Timing leaks of the nonce in ECDSA, EdDSA and DSA signatures
Helder Eijs reports

> Fix timing leaks of the nonce in ECDSA, EdDSA and DSA signatures The
> signing time depended on the secret nonce, enough to recover the
> private key with a lattice attack. - EC: scalars are encoded and
> processed on the full length of the order, and ec_mix_add() is
> constant time for the point at infinity (a zero digit). - DSA: g\^k
> uses k + q or k + 2q (fixed length) and mpz_powm_sec, or the
> library\'s Montgomery exponentiation without GMP. About 40% slower.
